About Us


Our comprehensive product portfolio includes:

  • Shelter systems (tents, hammocks)
  • Sleep solutions (sleeping bags, pads)
  • Outdoor furniture
  • Cooking and grilling equipment
  • Illumination gear
  • Specialized accessories


Strategically positioned to serve international B2B partners, we provide:

  • Comprehensive OEM/ODM capabilities with custom design services
  • Private label development solutions
  • Flexible minimum order quantity (MOQ) options
  • Dependable global logistics support

With robust supply chains and extensive manufacturing expertise, we currently serve key markets across America, Europe, Japan, Korea, Oceania, and the Gulf region. Our operations seamlessly integrate European technical innovation with Asian manufacturing efficiency, ensuring outstanding price-to-performance value.



In addition to manufacturing, we are actively growing our owned-brand distribution network globally. By partnering with us, you can take advantage of:

  • Expertise in product development
  • Rigorous quality assurance systems
  • Efficient lead times
  • In-depth knowledge of market-specific compliance

Reach out to our team to explore partnership opportunities customized to meet your market requirements.
